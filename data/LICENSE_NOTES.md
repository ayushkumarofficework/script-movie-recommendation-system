# Seed Script License & Source Notes

The seed scripts in `data/raw_scripts/` are intended for **local development
and demonstration of this recommendation system**. The licensing status of
each varies:

## Public-domain scripts (safe to ship verbatim)

- **Casablanca (1942)** — published 1942; in the public domain in most
  jurisdictions. Source: imsdb.com.
- **It's a Wonderful Life (1946)** — published 1946; in the public domain
  in most jurisdictions. Source: imsdb.com.

## Excerpt-only scripts (do not redistribute in full)

- **The Matrix (1999)** — © Warner Bros. We store only a trimmed excerpt
  sufficient to demonstrate the pipeline. Replace with rights-cleared text
  before any production deployment.
- **Inception (2010)** — © Warner Bros. Excerpt only.
- **Interstellar (2014)** — © Paramount / Warner Bros. Excerpt only.

## How to obtain scripts for additional movies

- imsdb.com
- dailyscript.com
- scriptslug.com
- awesome-film-screenplays (GitHub aggregator)

Always verify the licensing status of any script you ingest and respect
copyright.

EXT. CORNFIELD - DAY

A dust storm chews the horizon. COOPER (40s, calm under pressure) drives a beat-up pickup toward a column of black cloud.

INT. COOPER HOME - CONTINUOUS

MURPHY (10s, curious, angry) stares at a bookshelf. Books fall off, one by one, in time with an unseen rhythm.

COOPER
It happens because of gravity.

MURPHY
Then why doesn't the pencil fall?

COOPER
Because we're asking the wrong question.

INT. NASA FACILITY - NIGHT

PROFESSOR BRAND (60s, gentle), sits at a long table. A model of a wormhole hovers above it, drawn in failing light.

BRAND
We need a pilot. We need someone who has lost everything they were afraid of losing.

COOPER steps into the room, a patch still on his sleeve from farming.

COOPER
I'm your man.

EXT. ROCKET LAUNCH - DAY

The ENDURANCE rises. Inside, four crew members strap in. AMELIA (40s, wry) gives the camera a knowing look that the camera never catches.

INT. ENDURANCE - NIGHT

TARS, a tall monolith of brushed steel, addresses COOPER.

TARS
Honesty setting: ninety percent.

COOPER
What's wrong with one hundred percent?

TARS
You don't want a robot that always agrees with you.

EXT. MILLER'S PLANET - LATER (SUBJECTIVE)

Three hours of black sky go by. Then a wave, the size of a mountain, rises over the horizon.

AMELIA
We don't have time.

EXT. MANN'S PLANET - NIGHT

DR. MANN (50s, desperate) speaks into a cracked helmet.

MANN
I tried to save them. I tried.

EXT. ENDURANCE - CONTINUOUS

COOPER detaches a portion of the ship. The docking ring clicks loose. Mann, tethered, screams into the void as the planet turns beneath him.

INT. TARS DRONE CHAMBER - NIGHT

COOPER sits in a small room as the world outside folds into a tesseract of his daughter's bedroom, layered infinitely into itself.

COOPER (V.O.)
Don't let me leave, Murph.

EXT. COOPER HOME - DAY

MURPHY (90s) stands at a window. The bookshelf stops falling.

INT. HOSPITAL ROOM - LATER

COOPER opens his eyes. AMELIA stands at the foot of his bed.

COOPER
We agreed never to do this to each other. Never to stay.

AMELIA
And yet here we are.

She extends her hand. He takes it.

INT. PARIS CAFÉ - DAY

A man, COBB (40s, gaunt), sits alone. A toddler plays blocks at the next table. The child looks up. The face is wrong. The face is MAL (40s, sensuous, ruined).

COBB
Tell me... did you ever imagine it differently?

MAL
(laughing)
You keep saying that as if I had a choice.

He reaches for her. She dissolves into a flock of doves. The café shudders; gravity inverts. Mugs fall upward.

INT. SAITO'S PENTHOUSE - NIGHT

SAITO (60s) sits opposite COBB. Between them, a chess game is paused, mid-check.

SAITO
I want you to plant an idea in someone's head.

COBB
That's extraction, not inception.

SAITO
I have the resources to bring them back. To give you what you want.

A long beat. COBB touches the wedding ring on his left hand. The gold is warm, impossibly so.

COBB
Give me a team.

EXT. TOKYO SKYLINE - NIGHT

A train hurtles through neon. Cut to ARTHUR (30s, dependable) running along the outside of a moving building. He dives through a window into a hotel suite whose walls fold in on themselves like wet paper.

ARTHUR
We need a kick.

INT. LIMBO CITY - NIGHT

A city built from the wreckage of every dream. Bridges that lead nowhere. TOMMY GUN shots echo across empty piazzas.

COBB and MAL face each other in a ballroom. She is younger here, only 20, only ever 20.

MAL
You promised we'd grow old together.

COBB
I did.

MAL
Then why did you leave?

He has no answer. She slips through the floorboards.

EXT. SNOW FORTRESS - DAWN

A snowblower throws white across a mountainside. A team of operatives slides down a zip-line. ARIADNE (20s, bewildered) holds a bishop from a chess set.

ARIADNE
What is this place?

COBB
A memory of yours, if you want it to be.

ARIADNE
I don't want it to be. I want to wake up.

COBB
You are awake.

EXT. HOTEL HALLWAY - NIGHT (LOBBY LEVEL)

A spinning top wobbles on a table. The credits roll in the style of a dream unwinding.

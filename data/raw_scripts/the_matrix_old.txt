INT. METAVERSE LOADER - NIGHT

A digital city folds in on itself. HACKER, mid-30s, calm but wired, sits at a black terminal. The sky rewrites itself above his head.

HACKER
(quietly, to himself)
Am I supposed to mean something? Something more than a line of code?

He presses a key. A scrollable list of NAME FILES tumbles past — a thousand names, a thousand lives.

INT. ZION DOCK - CONTINUOUS

HOVERCRAFT "LOGOS" berths against a pillar of dripping basalt. Crew lock helmets. DOZENS of operatives check pulse-rifles.

Crews of men and women, decked out in patchwork armor, line the walls. The ship is a cathedral of pipes and cables.

MORPHEUS (60s, bald, robed in leather) steps forward. The room silences.

MORPHEUS
I imagine you're here for a reason. Many of us are.

He offers his hand.

MORPHEUS (CONT'D)
You have the look of a man who accepts what he sees because he is expecting to wake up. Believe me when I tell you — the pill you took was the blue kind.

HACKER
(swallowing)
I don't know what that means.

MORPHEUS
It means that what you think is true is no longer so. Most of what you call reality is a simulation.

A ripple passes through the room. The HACKER steadies himself against the railing.

HACKER
Then what's left?

MORPHEUS
(almost a whisper)
A choice.

INT. TRAINING ROOM - DAY (MONTHS LATER)

Hundreds of trainees fly between mirrored skyscrapers. The HACKER, now NEO, hovers mid-air, palms out, focused.

A GLOVE of white light races up his sleeve and disappears into the calluses of his hand.

NEO
I thought I knew kung fu.

MORPHEUS
You do. Now believe it.

A gong sounds. The room splits; an opponent materializes from light. NEO raises his hand. The opponent stops mid-air, suspended.

NEO (CONT'D)
How did I do that?

MORPHEUS
You didn't. The world around you did.

EXT. CITY STREET - NIGHT

A motorcade halts. AGENT SMITH, white shirt, sunglasses, steps out of a sedan. He smiles the way a child smiles before pulling wings off a fly.

AGENT SMITH
We have you surrounded.

NEO
Do you?

A hundred green digits shatter the sky. NEO leaps. The matrix bends around him.

INT. PHONE BOOTH - LATER

NEO and TRINITY (40s, scar across her cheek) stand shoulder to shoulder in a phone booth the size of a confessional.

TRINITY
I was going to let this place burn.

NEO
You didn't.

TRINITY
I wanted to.

A bell rings. The booth fills with light.

EXT. ROOFTOP - DAWN

NEO stands alone. The city radiates beneath him. Above him, a sky torn by data writes itself a new story.

NEO
(voiceover)
The only way out is through.

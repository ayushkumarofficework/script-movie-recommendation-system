EXT. CASABLANCA STREET MARKET - DAY

Refugees crowd around a visa officer's window. Money changes hands. YVONNE (30s, weary) pushes to the front. She meets RICK (40s, cynical, soft eyes) and waits.

INT. RICK'S CAFÉ AMÉRICAIN - NIGHT

A piano plays "As Time Goes By". Sam, the piano player, looks nervously at RICK. Rick nods.

LOU (30s, desperate) approaches RICK.

LOU
Rick, I need a letter of transit.

RICK
Everybody comes to Rick.

LOU
I have to leave Casablanca.

RICK
Then fly.

LOU
I can't. The police are watching.

RICK
Give them money. They take money.

INT. RICK'S OFFICE - LATER

LOU
I was in the resistance. I killed a German.

RICK
Then why worry?

LOU
My wife is here. She doesn't know.

A long silence. Rick pours two drinks. He slides one across the desk.

RICK
Here. To the resistance.

LOU
And to my wife.

INT. RICK'S CAFÉ AMÉRICAIN - NIGHT (MONTAGE)

A roulette wheel. A Nazi officer in uniform. A Bulgarian woman (YVONNE, again) gambling recklessly. RICK watches her from across the floor.

INT. RICK'S OFFICE - LATER THAT NIGHT

YVONNE
I want to leave with you, Rick.

RICK
I'm not going anywhere.

YVONNE
You are. You just don't know it yet.

INT. RICK'S OFFICE - CONTINUOUS

A woman appears in the doorway. ILSA (30s, beautiful, anguished). Rick freezes.

ILSA
Hello, Rick.

RICK
Hello, Ilsa.

ILSA
I thought I'd forgotten.

INT. PARIS - FLASHBACK, 1940

A rainy street. ILSA in a coat. RICK holding a letter of transit he doesn't yet know the cost of.

ILSA
I can't go with you.

RICK
Then why are you here?

ILSA
Because my husband is alive.

INT. RICK'S CAFÉ AMÉRICAIN - NIGHT (PRESENT)

A fight breaks out at the roulette table. Rick intervenes. He speaks to CAPTAIN RENAULT.

RENAULT
I'm shocked, shocked to find gambling here.

A waiter hands him his winnings.

RENAULT (CONT'D)
Oh, thank you.

EXT. CASABLANCA AIRFIELD - DAWN

Fog lifts. Rick and Ilsa stand at the foot of a small plane.

ILSA
You can still come.

RICK
You'll always have Paris.

The plane taxis. Rick walks back to Renault.

RENAULT
You were going to walk off with her.

RICK
I was.

RENAULT
What stopped you?

RICK
The beginning of a beautiful friendship.

The plane takes off into the fog.

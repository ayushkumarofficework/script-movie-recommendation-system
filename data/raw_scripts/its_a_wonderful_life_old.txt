EXT. BEDFORD FALLS - DAY

Snow falls on a small American town. Children race down the main street. GEORGE BAILEY (30s, harried) runs toward home.

INT. BAILEY HOME - CONTINUOUS

George bursts through the door. MARY (his wife, 20s, kind) catches him.

GEORGE
Where are the kids?

MARY
Where they always are. Imagining things.

INT. BAILEY BUILDING & LOAN - NIGHT

The office is empty. A drunk man, POTTER (60s, mean), sits across from George.

POTTER
You're worth more dead than alive.

GEORGE
That's my cue to leave.

EXT. BRIDGE - NIGHT

George stands on a bridge in the cold. He considers the river. A guardian angel, CLARENCE (50s, fumbling), appears.

CLARENCE
You've come to the wrong place.

GEORGE
Have I?

CLARENCE
You've come at exactly the right time.

INT. ALTERNATE BEDFORD FALLS - NIGHT (VISION)

A town without George. A graveyard where his house should be. A gravestone: CLARENCE ODDBODY, ANGEL SECOND CLASS.

CLARENCE
You see, George — you really did have a wonderful life.

GEORGE
(sinking)
Oh, my dear wife.

INT. BAILEY HOME - DAWN

George bursts through the front door, sobbing. Mary runs to him.

GEORGE
I saw what the town would be like without me.

MARY
And?

GEORGE
It's worse.

He laughs. He cries. He holds her. The children, snow-barefooted, run in from the street.

GEORGE (CONT'D)
I want to live again.

MARY
Then live.

INT. BAILEY BUILDING & LOAN - MORNING

A banker signs a document. George holds a book: "Tom Sawyer". He laughs. He cries. He leaves the office with a spring in his step.

EXT. BEDFORD FALLS STREET - CONTINUOUS

The whole town pours out. A bell rings. Money changes hands. A toast: "To George, the richest man in town."

George raises his glass.

GEORGE
To my family. To my town. To my life.

The snow falls softly. Clarence receives his wings.
